Step-by-Step Client Setup
In MetaTrader 5, the client clicks File $\rightarrow$ Open Data Folder.
They open the MQL5 folder, then open the Presets folder.
They copy/paste the .set files into that Presets folder.
When they drag the EA onto a chart, they go to the Inputs tab, click Load, and MT5 automatically opens the Presets folder where they can pick the right file!

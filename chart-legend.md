# MNS Indicator - Quick Reference & Legend

A simple cheat-sheet explaining all on-chart symbols, abbreviations, and color codes for the visual elements.

---

## 1. Structural Break Lines
These lines mark trend continuations and market structure shifts.

* **BOS (Break of Structure)**
  * *Meaning:* Trend continuation. Price has broken a major swing high/low.
  * *Bullish BOS:* **Lime Green** dashed line.
  * *Bearish BOS:* **Red** dashed line.
  * *Visual Weight:* The most recent BOS line is drawn at double thickness.
* **iBOS (Internal BOS)**
  * *Meaning:* Structure continuation within the internal/minor swings.
  * *Bullish iBOS:* **Teal** dashed line.
  * *Bearish iBOS:* **Dark Magenta** dashed line.
* **CHoCH (Change of Character)**
  * *Meaning:* The first sign of a market structure shift/reversal.
  * *CHoCH:* **Orange** dotted line.

---

## 2. Liquidity Levels
These lines mark areas where stops and buy/sell limit orders rest.

* **BSL (Buy Side Liquidity)**
  * *Meaning:* Resting buy stops above a major swing high.
  * *Color:* **Dodger Blue** dashed line.
* **SSL (Sell Side Liquidity)**
  * *Meaning:* Resting sell stops below a major swing low.
  * *Color:* **Tomato Red** dashed line.
* **EQH (Equal Highs)**
  * *Meaning:* Multiple highs at/near the same price (stronger liquidity draw).
  * *Color:* **Light Sky Blue** dashed line.
* **EQL (Equal Lows)**
  * *Meaning:* Multiple lows at/near the same price (stronger liquidity draw).
  * *Color:* **Light Coral** dashed line.
* **Swept**
  * *Meaning:* Price has traded past a liquidity pool level, cleaning out the stops.
  * *Color:* **Slate Gray** dotted line.

---

## 3. Delivery Objective
* **DOL / TAKE PROFIT (Draw on Liquidity)**
  * *Meaning:* The primary price target the market is currently drawn towards.
  * *Color:* **Gold** dotted line.

---

## 4. Points of Interest (POIs)
These are key zones (Fair Value Gaps and Order Blocks) where the engine expects reaction.

* **Bullish POIs (OB / FVG)**
  * *Meaning:* Buy zones.
  * *Color:* Dark filled **Green** box.
* **Bearish POIs (OB / FVG)**
  * *Meaning:* Sell zones.
  * *Color:* Dark filled **Red** box.
* *Active vs Mitigated:* Active POI borders are drawn thicker (width 2). When price enters/mitigates the zone, its border changes to a thin dotted line (width 1) and fades.

---

## 5. Swing Point Markers
These are the small dots indicating confirmed market swings.

* **External Swings (Major Structure)**
  * *Visual:* **Size 3** dot (filled circle).
  * *Highs:* **Lime Green** dot above the candle wick.
  * *Lows:* **Red** dot below the candle wick.
* **Internal Swings (Minor Structure)**
  * *Visual:* **Size 2** dot (filled circle).
  * *Highs:* **Teal** dot above the candle wick.
  * *Lows:* **Dark Magenta** dot below the candle wick.
